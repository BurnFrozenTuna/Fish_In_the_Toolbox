Before you use it

An experimental tool for light final tone and level adjustments. It does not restore clipped audio or separate muddy instruments. Suitable settings vary by track.

Meters show sample peak and whole-track RMS, not LUFS or true peak. MP3 encoding can increase peaks slightly. The original file is unchanged.

Decoding and processing require available memory. Compare with the original before saving.
