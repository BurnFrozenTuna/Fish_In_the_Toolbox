What gets removed

MP4/M4A: clears udta/meta/UUID tags and padding; clears creation/modification times, track names and encoder-name fields. File size may stay the same.

Does not remove media content, watermarks, information inside encoded streams, or every nonstandard tag. Playback information such as codec, duration and resolution remains. This is not a guarantee of full anonymization.

Format references: ID3 · FLAC · MP4 / QuickTime
